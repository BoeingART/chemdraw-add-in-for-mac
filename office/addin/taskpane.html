Installation and troubleshooting

Open ChemOffice from Applications. Its menu bar app runs the local editing helper and this panel.

Find the entry under Home → Add-ins → Developer Add-ins → ChemOffice for Mac. Once activated, the ChemOffice ribbon tab also opens it.

Use your existing Office installation. Open the blue C entry inside each app to activate the panel. If an automated check labels the entry disabled, confirm it with an actual foreground click.

If the panel will not load, choose Check Installation in the ChemOffice menu. A fresh installation also needs the Mac to trust the local localhost certificate.

This local preview automatically selects the newest installed ChemDraw and needs the desktop Office applications. It creates managed SVG drawings with full source, not Windows OLE objects.
