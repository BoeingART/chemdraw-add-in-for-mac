Installation and troubleshooting

Open ChemDraw Add-in for Mac from Applications or its Dock icon. Its configuration window shows permissions and controls the local editing helper.

Find the entry under Home → Add-ins → Developer Add-ins → ChemDraw Add-in for Mac. Once activated, the ChemDraw Add-in for Mac ribbon tab also opens it.

Use your existing Office installation. Open the blue C entry inside each app to activate the panel. If an automated check labels the entry disabled, confirm it with an actual foreground click.

If the panel will not load, open the configuration window and choose 检查安装. A fresh installation also needs the Mac to trust the local localhost certificate.

This local preview automatically selects the newest installed ChemDraw and needs the desktop Office applications. It creates managed SVG drawings with full editable source.
