ChemDraw Add-in for Mac

安装
1. 将 ChemDraw Add-in for Mac.app 拖到旁边的 Applications 文件夹。
2. 打开应用，按“设置加载项”的提示完成首次授权。程序会检查已安装的 Word、PowerPoint、Excel，并自动启动助手。

更新
在应用中选择“检查更新”，确认后自动下载、更新并重新打开。已有授权和编辑草稿继续保留；正在编辑时，更新会等待编辑结束。

使用
在 ChemDraw 中复制结构，到 Word、PowerPoint 或 Excel 中粘贴。双击继续编辑，⌘S 或菜单“保存”同步图片，关闭绘图后返回原文档。Office 文档由你决定何时保存。

Installation
1. Drag ChemDraw Add-in for Mac.app into the adjacent Applications folder.
2. Open the app and complete the first-run permission prompts. Setup checks installed Word, PowerPoint and Excel apps and starts the helper automatically.

Updates
Choose Check for Updates in the app. After confirmation, it downloads, replaces the app and reopens automatically. Existing permissions and editing drafts are retained. Active edits finish before installation continues.

Usage
Copy a structure in ChemDraw and paste into Word, PowerPoint or Excel. Double-click to edit, save to synchronize the figure, and close the drawing to return to its Office document. Save the Office document when you choose.
