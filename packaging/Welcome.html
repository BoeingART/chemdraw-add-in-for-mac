ChemOffice for Mac

Version 0.1.2 · Apple Silicon + Intel · macOS 12 or later

将原生 ChemOffice 应用安装到“应用程序”，无需 Python、Homebrew 或开发工具。需另行安装并激活 ChemDraw、Word 和 PowerPoint。

安装完成后自动打开应用。首次启动会安装 Office 加载项，提示授权本机 localhost 证书与辅助功能；受系统保护的 Office 文件夹可能需要在 Finder 中复制加载项。

This package is unsigned and has not been notarized by Apple. Use System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway if macOS blocks it. The native Word workflow passed with ChemDraw 26 and Word 16.114.1 on macOS 27.0.1. Native PowerPoint and ChemDraw 22 end-to-end acceptance remain pending.
