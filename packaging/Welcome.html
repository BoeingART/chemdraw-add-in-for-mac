ChemDraw Add-in for Mac

Version 1.5.7 · Apple Silicon + Intel · macOS 12 or later

Paste structures from multi-page ChemDraw documents. Opening an edit restores the drawing’s saved settings and selects native Mac ChemDraw.

Choose Check for Updates and confirm to download, replace and reopen the app automatically.

Add-in Setup opens automatically after installation. Allow Office file access when prompted, then complete the five checks to start using the app.

Existing drawings, connection certificates, and recovery drafts are retained. When upgrading from an older version, confirm Accessibility and Automation access when prompted.

Finish editing and quit the app before upgrading.
