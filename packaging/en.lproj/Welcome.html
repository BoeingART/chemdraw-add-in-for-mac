ChemDraw Add-in for Mac

Version 1.3.4 · Apple Silicon + Intel · macOS 12 or later

The app windows, Office panel, ribbon, and installation instructions follow your preferred system language in Chinese or English.

After changing your preferred system language, quit the app from its menu bar, then reopen the app and Office to switch the interface language.

Add-in Setup opens automatically after installation. Allow Office file access when prompted, then complete the four checks to start using the app.

Existing drawings, connection certificates, and recovery drafts are retained. When upgrading from an older version, confirm Accessibility and Automation access when prompted.

Finish editing and quit the app before upgrading.
