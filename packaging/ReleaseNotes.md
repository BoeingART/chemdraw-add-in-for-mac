## ChemDraw Add-in for Mac 1.5.4

### 中文

- 修复ChemDraw 26保存和关闭回写时弹出临时绘图窗口的问题，预览改为后台生成。
- 修复直接粘贴时带入整页空白的问题，预览按结构、文字和图片的实际范围裁切，保留矢量和可编辑源数据。
- 保留已有授权和编辑草稿。完成当前编辑后，可通过应用内“检查更新”升级。

### English

- Generate ChemDraw 26 previews in the background, removing temporary drawing windows during save and close/write-back.
- Crop pasted previews to the actual structures, text and pictures, preserving vector artwork and editable source.
- Retain existing permissions and editing drafts. Finish editing, then choose Check for Updates in the app to upgrade.
