## ChemDraw Add-in for Mac 1.5.2

### 中文

- 修复ChemDraw 26 for Mac粘贴结构时无法生成预览的问题，继续使用PDF生成可编辑矢量图片。
- 粘贴、保存同步和关闭回写统一使用独立结构副本导出预览，保留原编辑窗口及随后继续输入的修改。
- 保留空白结构的可编辑画布；PDF导出失败时显示具体原因和重试操作。
- 安装后重新复制ChemDraw结构并粘贴；Office文档仍由你决定何时保存。

### English

- Fix preview generation when pasting structures from ChemDraw 26 for Mac, retaining PDF-based editable vector artwork.
- Use independent structure copies for paste, save synchronization and close/write-back previews, preserving the original editor and subsequent input.
- Keep empty drawings editable and show the specific PDF-export failure with retry instructions.
- After installation, copy the structure from ChemDraw again and paste it. Save Office documents when you choose.
