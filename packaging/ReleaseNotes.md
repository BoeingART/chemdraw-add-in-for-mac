## ChemDraw Add-in for Mac 1.5.5

### 中文

- 修复在ChemDraw中快速保存后立即关闭时，修改未回写Office图片的问题。
- 关闭发生在后台预览生成期间时，自动同步最后保存的内容，再返回原Office文档。
- 改善保存和关闭的响应，保留已有授权与编辑草稿。完成当前编辑后，可通过应用内“检查更新”升级。

### English

- Fix Office images missing edits when a ChemDraw drawing is saved and immediately closed.
- Synchronize the latest saved content when the editor closes during background preview generation, then return to the original Office document.
- Improve save and close responsiveness while retaining existing permissions and editing drafts. Finish editing, then choose Check for Updates to upgrade.
