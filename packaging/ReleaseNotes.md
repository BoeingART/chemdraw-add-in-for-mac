## ChemDraw Add-in for Mac 1.5.1

### 中文

- 同步新的应用图标、Office面板横幅和菜单栏图标，应用主窗口保持紧凑布局。
- 检查更新并确认后，应用自动下载、替换并重新打开，保留已有授权、文档连接和编辑草稿。
- 正在编辑结构时等待编辑结束后更新；完成后清理本次更新的临时文件。
- 保留Word、PowerPoint、Excel的可编辑矢量结构、保存同步和关闭回写。

### English

- Refresh the application icon, Office panel banner and menu-bar artwork, keeping the application window compact.
- Check for updates and confirm to download, replace and reopen automatically, retaining existing permissions, document connections and editing drafts.
- Active drawing edits finish before installation; completed update files are removed automatically.
- Preserve editable vector chemistry, save synchronization and close/write-back in Word, PowerPoint and Excel.
