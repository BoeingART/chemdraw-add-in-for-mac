ChemOffice for Mac is an independent experiment to make chemical drawings feel at home in Microsoft Word and PowerPoint on a Mac. Keep using the ChemDraw you know, with editable source carried alongside your figure.

> **An honest early preview.** The intended experience is double-click, edit, close, and return. Live compatibility results will be recorded as this prototype is tested. This is not yet a promise of Windows OLE compatibility.

## A little less friction

- **Stay with ChemDraw.** Use your installed, licensed desktop application.
- **Keep the chemistry.** Preserve editable source, not just a picture.
- **Keep your place.** Return to the original document and figure.
- **Stay on your Mac.** The prototype is designed around local files and local applications.

## Before you begin

You will need Microsoft Word and PowerPoint for Mac, an activated ChemDraw installation, and permission for the helper to communicate with these applications.

Installation and tested behavior are being established in this prototype. See [Live test results](docs/live-test-results.md) for the current evidence and limitations. Use copies of your documents during early testing.

## Your work travels with your figure

The design pairs a preview with editable ChemDraw source. A recipient can view the preview without this helper; editing requires a compatible ChemDraw installation and the integration. Existing Windows OLE objects are a separate compatibility case.
